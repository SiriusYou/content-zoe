## Facts and observations

- AMA reports that more than 80 percent of physicians surveyed use AI professionally. The 2026 survey was fielded in January and February 2026 across a broad physician respondent pool.
- The AMA survey supports a practitioner-exposure story: AI is appearing in research support, documentation, discharge instructions, administrative work, and other professional tasks.
- The AMA source also supports a training-gap story. Physicians report wanting more AI education and clearer guidance for safe use.
- Mount Sinai's Dragon Copilot rollout is an example of an academic medical center deploying ambient/voice clinical workflow AI at enterprise scale. It should be treated as a provider-specific rollout, not as proof of universal health-system adoption.

## W28 framing

- Reader-safe headline: physician and provider exposure to AI is broadening, but training, workflow fit, and governance remain the gating issues.
- Avoid treating survey adoption as audited production deployment. The AMA evidence is self-reported.
- Avoid treating one academic medical center rollout as representative of all provider organizations.
