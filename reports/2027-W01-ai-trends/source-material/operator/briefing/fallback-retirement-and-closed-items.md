# Fallback, retirement, and closed items

## Fallback readiness

A review queue should preserve fallback and contingency questions when AI-enabled workflows continue, narrow, pause, or stop. ONC SAFER and contingency-planning material supports the operational need to understand how systems, workflows, maintenance, downtime, and recovery are handled.

The report may describe fallback questions such as:

- what happens if the AI-enabled function is unavailable;
- who switches to fallback;
- how output is reconciled;
- whether users know the fallback path;
- whether the fallback path has been reviewed or tested;
- whether a paused or retired workflow leaves dependencies.

## Retirement and closed status

Closed should mean intentionally closed, not forgotten. Retired or archived items may still need records when dependencies, stored output, user communication, vendor transition, monitoring review, incident review, or replacement readiness remains open.

The report may recommend that the queue distinguish retired, archived, and closed. It should not claim any specific retention or legal duty.

## Boundary

Fallback and retirement fields can strengthen continuity. They do not prove resilience, safety, compliance, or operational maturity.
