# Owner and decision rights

## Owner follow-up

A review queue only works if each item can find an accountable owner or owner group. Depending on the workflow, that owner may be clinical, operational, technical, safety, privacy, security, analytics, revenue-cycle, informatics, or governance-based.

The report may say that the start of the year is a useful moment to confirm whether an owner still exists, still accepts responsibility, and still has a route to escalate concerns.

## Decision rights

Open items should connect to decisions. A queue entry should make clear what kind of decision is needed and who can make or route that decision.

Possible decision categories include:

- continue with owner confirmation;
- continue with monitoring review;
- request evidence;
- validate locally;
- clarify use boundary;
- update source attributes or user notice;
- narrow use;
- pause use;
- retire or archive;
- reopen governance review;
- escalate for decision.

## Avoid invented local structure

Do not invent committee names, authority maps, due dates, or local escalation paths. The report may describe what a local queue should capture; it cannot provide local answers.
