# Recommendation versus directive output

## Editorial use

Use this file to keep the report precise about decision support. The draft should distinguish outputs that inform a human decision from outputs that direct, substitute for, or obscure the basis of judgment. It should not turn this distinction into legal advice or a universal regulatory classification.

## Source basis

- FDA Clinical Decision Support Software materials discuss criteria for non-device CDS, including whether software provides recommendations rather than a specific output or directive and whether the basis is available so a health care professional does not rely primarily on the recommendation.
- FDA software-function materials emphasize function-specific review rather than treating every software feature as the same category.
- ONC / HealthIT.gov DSI materials support structured source attributes and visibility into predictive decision support intervention information in covered certified-health-IT contexts.
- AMA materials support an assistive framing for AI and continued attention to professional responsibility and transparency.

## Reader-safe claims

- A healthcare AI output should be labeled operationally: is it offering information, ranking options, recommending an action, pre-populating a record, or triggering a process?
- The basis for a recommendation matters because reviewers need to know whether they are evaluating evidence, interpreting a summary, or being pushed toward a directive.
- Public device, software, and certified-health-IT materials can help route review, but the local organization still has to understand intended use, workflow effect, and reliance pattern.
- A decision-support output can still create pressure if it appears as a default, hides alternatives, or makes override difficult.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not give a legal classification for any specific product or workflow.
- Do not imply that all decision support is non-device, low-risk, or outside oversight.
- Do not imply that a displayed basis guarantees meaningful human review.
- Do not claim source attributes or decision-support records prove safety, fairness, effectiveness, or compliance.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support careful routing questions about recommendation, directive output, basis for review, and function-specific context. They do not establish a legal conclusion or prove local behavior.
