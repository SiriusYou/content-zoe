# Vendor-embedded and agent-like workflow boundaries

## Editorial use

Use this file to cover AI that arrives inside vendor platforms, EHR configuration, productivity tools, outsourced workflows, and agent-like automation. The report should avoid hype. It should ask how organizations know what the feature can do, what changed after an update, what data or actions it can access, and when a local review must happen before expanded use.

## Source basis

- FDA software-function materials support function-specific review and caution against treating every software capability as the same category.
- ONC / HealthIT.gov DSI materials support source-attribute visibility for certain covered decision-support contexts.
- NIST AI RMF supports mapping third-party, organizational, and lifecycle risk contexts.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance announcement supports adaptable governance, validation, monitoring, and use in health systems.
- AMA materials note that AI is used in medical-device and non-device health care contexts, including administration and burden-reduction use cases, while practical guidance remains necessary.

## Reader-safe claims

- Embedded AI should be reviewed when a vendor update changes what the tool can read, write, recommend, route, or trigger.
- Agent-like workflows need scoped permissions, approval points, logs, fallback paths, and clear ownership before they affect patient-facing, clinician-facing, record-facing, or operational actions.
- Procurement and product-management records should capture what the vendor says the feature does, what the customer can configure, what changes require notice, and what evidence is available for local review.
- Shadow or informal use matters when general-purpose tools start shaping documentation, communication, scheduling, triage, billing, or administrative decisions.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim any named vendor or product is safe, unsafe, compliant, or noncompliant.
- Do not imply agentic workflow automation is mature in healthcare.
- Do not claim vendor assurances replace local review.
- Do not equate a vendor feature label with a complete governance record.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support third-party and embedded-workflow review questions. They do not include vendor-specific contracts, product evidence, local configuration evidence, audit rights, or performance results.
