# Workaround signal model

## Core idea

A healthcare AI workaround is a visible or invisible way users adapt around an AI-enabled output. It may include bypassing the output, duplicating the work elsewhere, delaying action, reinterpreting the result, relying on a non-AI path, or using the output only after informal checks.

The workaround matters because it may point to a workflow-fit problem, trust problem, missing-context problem, evidence gap, escalation gap, fallback issue, or user-burden problem. It may also be reasonable in a specific setting. The article should treat workarounds as signals to review, not as automatic proof of user error or model failure.

## What a useful workaround record can include

- workflow or AI-enabled function;
- output type;
- intended users;
- observed workaround type;
- setting;
- timing;
- reason category when known;
- affected next action;
- monitoring contact;
- owner group;
- fallback route;
- escalation route;
- review trigger.

These fields are useful only when they help route action. A workaround count without context can become a misleading metric.

## What a workaround log is not

A workaround log is not proof of safety, non-safety, compliance, noncompliance, effectiveness, fairness, patient trust, resilience, maturity, or outcome improvement. It is also not a substitute for clinical judgment, legal analysis, local validation, privacy review, cybersecurity review, professional review, labor review, or patient-safety review.

The article may argue that workaround visibility makes governance questions easier to find. It must not claim that the log resolves those questions.
