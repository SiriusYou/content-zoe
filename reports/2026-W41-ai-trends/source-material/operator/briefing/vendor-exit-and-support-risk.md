# Vendor exit and support risk

## Editorial use

Use this file to frame vendor exit as part of resilience. Healthcare AI can depend on vendor support, cloud services, data feeds, model updates, documentation, and contract terms. The report should discuss exit planning without ranking vendors or claiming a specific vendor risk.

## Source basis to cite cautiously

- HHS 405(d), HICP, and HC3 materials support operational resilience, cybersecurity, and third-party risk awareness in healthcare.
- NIST AI RMF supports third-party and supply-chain risk documentation where relevant.
- Joint Commission responsible health-data materials support data controls, permitted use, and governance where data is transferred or reused.
- WHO health AI guidance supports accountability across public and private actors.

## Supported claims

- Vendor exit planning should address data return or deletion, model or service replacement, audit logs, support handoff, contract termination, and communication to affected users.
- Vendor support changes can affect operations even when the model itself is unchanged.
- Exit planning should include evidence about what functions depend on the vendor and how service disruption would be handled.

## Unsafe claims

- Do not claim any vendor is secure, insecure, reliable, or unreliable.
- Do not imply a vendor support change occurred.
- Do not treat contract language as proof that exit controls work in practice.

## Evidence warning

The staged sources support vendor-exit planning as a resilience duty. They do not provide evidence about any specific vendor's performance or security posture.
