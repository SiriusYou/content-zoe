# Validation after change

## Editorial use

Use this file to keep the report focused on evidence refresh after updates. The report should say that launch validation can become stale when the model, data, interface, workflow, or downstream use changes.

## Source basis

- FDA PCCP materials support source-bounded planning for certain AI-enabled device software changes.
- ONC DSI and SAFER materials support decision-support visibility, implementation, validation, maintenance, and safe use of health IT.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance supports local validation, monitoring, governance, and context-specific use.
- NIST AI RMF supports measuring and managing risks over the lifecycle.

## Reader-safe claims

- Update review should ask which parts of local validation need to be refreshed.
- Refreshed validation can examine the affected workflow, user group, patient group, setting, source data, output behavior, false positives, false negatives, overrides, timing, and handoffs.
- The record should distinguish a vendor's update evidence from local evidence that the update still fits the organization's workflow.
- If a change touches subgroup behavior, access burden, fallback readiness, or near-miss patterns, those prior review threads may need to be reopened.
- If validation cannot be refreshed before release, the record should say what interim monitoring or narrowed use is planned.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim validation proves safety, effectiveness, compliance, or fairness.
- Do not claim vendor evidence replaces local validation.
- Do not invent performance metrics or subgroup results.
- Do not imply every update needs a full relaunch review.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support validation-refresh questions. They do not include local validation data, performance metrics, subgroup analyses, incident logs, or outcome evidence.
