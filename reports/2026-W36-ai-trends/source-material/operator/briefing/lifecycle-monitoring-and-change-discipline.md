## Facts and observations

- FDA AI-enabled device software materials support the idea that AI accountability continues after initial review.
- Predetermined change control planning provides a concrete vocabulary for anticipated changes, modification protocols, performance evaluation, and bounded updates.
- Lifecycle management materials support ongoing monitoring and postmarket discipline in regulated device software.
- These sources are useful as a disciplined example of change governance.
- They do not make every healthcare workflow AI tool equivalent to regulated AI-enabled device software.

## W36 framing

- Reader-safe headline direction: healthcare AI governance needs monitoring of change, not only launch approval.
- Use device-software materials as a bounded example of lifecycle discipline.
- Keep the regulatory boundary explicit.

## Evidence limits

- These sources are strongest for regulated device software.
- They do not validate ambient documentation tools, administrative models, non-device workflow assistants, or local deployments.
