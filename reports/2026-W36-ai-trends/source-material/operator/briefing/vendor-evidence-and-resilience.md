## Facts and observations

- Third-party AI risk guidance supports treating healthcare AI procurement as supply-chain and evidence-management work.
- Vendor evidence, contract terms, data-flow visibility, testing claims, monitoring obligations, and incident response are governance inputs.
- Health-sector cybersecurity guidance connects cyber resilience with operational continuity and patient safety.
- NIST AI RMF gives vocabulary for governing, mapping, measuring, and managing AI risks.
- These materials support a report about vendor evidence becoming part of AI assurance.
- They do not prove that a vendor, framework, contract, or organization is resilient in practice.

## W36 framing

- Reader-safe headline direction: AI assurance depends on what vendors can prove and what health systems can monitor.
- Connect supply-chain transparency, cyber resilience, procurement discipline, and incident response.
- Avoid treating framework adoption or contract language as proof of maturity.

## Evidence limits

- These references support governance requirements and risk categories.
- They do not validate vendor performance, security posture, compliance, or incident readiness.
