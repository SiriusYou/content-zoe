## Facts and observations

- AMA materials support human-accountability framing: AI should augment professionals and requires training, review, escalation, and documentation.
- Physician adoption signals support governance urgency but do not prove safe implementation.
- HIPAA and de-identification references provide privacy boundaries and methods; they are not evidence of AI deployment compliance.
- WHO and OECD references support accountable, transparent, human-centered, context-sensitive governance language.
- These materials support a cautious report about human responsibility and privacy boundaries.
- They do not prove patient benefit, local compliance, vendor quality, or operational readiness.

## W36 framing

- Reader-safe headline direction: AI assurance still depends on human review and privacy discipline.
- Connect physician exposure to workflow accountability, escalation, and documentation.
- Treat privacy and ethics frameworks as guardrails, not proof of readiness.

## Evidence limits

- These references support governance boundaries and accountability language.
- They are not clinical outcome studies, compliance audits, or vendor-performance assessments.
