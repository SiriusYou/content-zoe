# Local subgroup and context review

## Editorial use

Use this file to make W49 about local equity review. The report should argue that fairness cannot be treated as a static property of a model or vendor file. The practical question is whether the AI-enabled workflow behaves differently for the local people, settings, access paths, and constraints it now touches.

## Source basis

- NIST AI RMF supports risk management tied to context, mapping, measurement, management, roles, monitoring, and affected communities.
- WHO ethics and governance material supports protection, inclusiveness, human autonomy, transparency, accountability, and public-interest framing for health AI.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance supports local validation, governance, monitoring, and organizational context.
- eCFR 45 CFR 92.210 and HHS OCR Section 1557 material provide nondiscrimination context for patient care decision support tools. Use only as source context, not as legal advice.

## Reader-safe claims

- Local equity review should begin by naming the actual workflow, patient population, care setting, user group, access path, and decision point.
- The review should ask who is included, who is missed, who is delayed, who is escalated, who receives extra documentation burden, and who needs a fallback path.
- A subgroup list should be tied to local risk and available evidence, not copied mechanically from a vendor document.
- Risk can differ by setting, language, disability, age, geography, digital access, payer or coverage path, and handoff structure.
- Equity review should include the people responsible for clinical workflow, patient access, quality, compliance, IT, data governance, and escalation.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim the staged material proves any subgroup has been harmed or helped.
- Do not claim a specific legal obligation beyond the cited source context.
- Do not imply that every subgroup question can be answered with the same metric.
- Do not present demographic labels as a substitute for workflow review.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support a local review frame. They do not include local subgroup outcomes, utilization logs, complaint patterns, or implementation evidence.
