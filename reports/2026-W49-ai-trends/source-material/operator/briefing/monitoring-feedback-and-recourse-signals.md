# Monitoring, feedback, and recourse signals

## Editorial use

Use this file to frame post-launch monitoring as an equity input. The report should argue that equity review needs channels for observing differential behavior and for receiving corrections from patients and staff when the workflow misses context.

## Source basis

- NIST AI RMF supports monitoring, measurement, management, documentation, and response over the AI lifecycle.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance supports monitoring and governance adapted to organizational context.
- WHO health-AI ethics material supports accountability, transparency, human oversight, and protection of affected people.
- ONC Decision Support Interventions material supports visibility into intervention references, logic, and source attributes.

## Reader-safe claims

- Monitoring should ask whether false positives, false negatives, overrides, delays, escalations, missed referrals, or documentation burdens differ across relevant local groups or settings.
- Feedback channels should include clinical users, operations teams, patient access teams, compliance or quality staff, and, where appropriate, patient-facing complaint or appeal paths.
- Recourse should be understandable enough that a person knows how to question, correct, escalate, or bypass the AI-enabled workflow.
- A dashboard is not enough if it cannot show who is affected, what changed, and what action followed.
- The review record should name unresolved evidence gaps and assign an owner for follow-up.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim monitoring proves fairness or safety.
- Do not claim complaints or appeals are complete measures of harm.
- Do not claim absence of complaints proves absence of inequity.
- Do not invent local complaints, appeal volumes, override rates, or subgroup performance.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support monitoring, feedback, and recourse questions. They do not include local logs, appeals, patient complaints, incident reports, measured outcomes, or mitigation effectiveness data.
