# Owner-of-record model

## Core idea

An AI-enabled workflow should have an owner-of-record for governance follow-up. The owner-of-record is not necessarily the vendor, the model developer, or the person who uses the system most often. It is the accountable person or group that can move the review item forward.

The owner should connect to the workflow and its local use boundary. A model name alone is not enough because the same tool or function may appear in different settings, users, outputs, or downstream actions.

## Useful owner fields

The report may describe fields such as:

- workflow or AI-enabled function;
- owner group;
- clinical or operational setting;
- intended users;
- approved or reviewed use boundary;
- decision rights;
- review responsibility;
- monitoring responsibility;
- fallback contact;
- escalation route;
- last owner-confirmation date when one exists.

## Boundary

Owner assignment can make governance work routeable. It does not prove that the workflow is safe, compliant, effective, fair, mature, or locally validated.
