# Unknown, disputed, and outdated ownership

## Unknown ownership

Unknown ownership should remain visible. If no owner is known, the item should not be treated as cleared. The next action may be owner discovery or escalation to the group responsible for assigning ownership.

## Disputed ownership

Disputed ownership can happen when a workflow crosses clinical, operational, technical, vendor, or administrative boundaries. The report may say that disputed ownership should be recorded as a review problem rather than hidden behind a generic owner field.

## Outdated ownership

Ownership can become stale when a workflow changes, staff roles change, vendor relationships change, use expands, monitoring responsibility shifts, or a function is paused, retired, or replaced. Owner confirmation should be repeated when those conditions apply.

## Boundary

Unknown, disputed, or outdated ownership is a review signal. It is not proof that the workflow is unsafe or noncompliant.
