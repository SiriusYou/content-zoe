# Decision rights and next actions

## Why decision rights matter

An owner field is weak if it does not say what the owner can decide or route. A review item should show whether the owner can continue, narrow, pause, retire, reopen review, request evidence, validate locally, update monitoring, coordinate fallback, or escalate for decision.

## Next-action categories

The report may use categories such as:

- confirm owner;
- gather evidence;
- review monitoring;
- validate locally;
- clarify use boundary;
- update source attributes;
- update training or user notice;
- review fallback;
- narrow or pause use;
- retire or archive;
- reopen governance review;
- escalate for decision.

These are possible action categories, not universal requirements.

## Split ownership

Some review items may need shared responsibility across clinical, operational, technical, safety, privacy, security, informatics, analytics, revenue-cycle, vendor-management, or governance groups. The queue should still show who owns the next action and who must be consulted.

## Boundary

Decision-right labels make review work easier to route. They do not prove legal authority, regulatory sufficiency, or good governance by themselves.
