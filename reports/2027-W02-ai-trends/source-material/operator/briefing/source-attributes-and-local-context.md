# Source attributes and local context

## Why ownership needs context

Ownership is stronger when it is tied to source attributes and local workflow context. Decision-support materials support attention to source attributes, references, logic visibility, and implementation information where available. Risk-management and health IT safety materials support context mapping, monitoring, contingency planning, and lifecycle review.

The report may say that an owner record should link to available source attributes, local evidence, monitoring, workflow context, approved use boundary, and fallback information. In public prose, refer to "the source basis" or "available governance sources" when discussing evidence limits.

## Vendor evidence versus local evidence

Owner records should help distinguish vendor evidence from local evidence. Vendor material may inform review, but it does not answer every local workflow question by itself.

## Bounded device context

FDA AI- or ML-enabled medical device material can provide bounded device-context information for some functions. It does not define the whole healthcare AI landscape or replace local owner assignment.

## Boundary

Source attributes and local context can improve accountability. They do not prove completeness, safety, compliance, effectiveness, fairness, trust, outcomes, or maturity.
