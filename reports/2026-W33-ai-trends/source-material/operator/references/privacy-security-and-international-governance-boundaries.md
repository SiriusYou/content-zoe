## Facts and observations

- HIPAA and de-identification references are privacy boundary material; they should not be used as shorthand for full AI compliance.
- NIST AI RMF supplies cross-sector risk-management vocabulary and lifecycle framing.
- OECD AI principles and WHO health-AI ethics materials emphasize human-centered, trustworthy, accountable, and context-sensitive governance.
- These sources support boundary setting and cautious governance language.
- They do not prove that any local implementation is compliant, equitable, safe, or effective.

## W33 framing

- Reader-safe headline: privacy and governance frameworks define boundaries for responsible AI scaling.
- Use these references to explain why governance claims should stay modest.
- Avoid treating framework alignment as evidence of deployment success.

## Evidence limits

- These are governance and privacy references, not empirical healthcare-AI outcome studies.
- They should support caution, not hype.
