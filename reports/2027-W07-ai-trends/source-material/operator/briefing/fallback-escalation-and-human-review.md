# Fallback, escalation, and human review

## Why uncertainty needs a route

Uncertainty becomes operationally important when the reader must decide what to do next. If a report, flag, draft, or recommendation is uncertain but gives no fallback route, escalation route, or review owner, the uncertainty may be noticed without being acted on.

The article may argue that an uncertainty label should connect limits to a next action. It must not claim that any specific fallback route is legally sufficient, clinically required, or universally appropriate.

## Route types the article may name

- human review before action;
- fallback to a non-AI workflow;
- escalation to an owner group;
- pause until missing context is resolved;
- documentation of why the output was not used;
- monitoring review when uncertainty recurs.

## Boundary

Do not turn these route types into compliance advice. Present them as governance questions supported by public source families, not as local requirements.
