## Facts and observations

- HHS describes a OneHHS approach to AI and frames AI governance around trust, practical impact, and responsible use across the department and health sector.
- HTI-1 and HealthIT.gov decision-support intervention material show that algorithm transparency is becoming part of certified health IT expectations.
- FDA's AI-enabled medical device list is a transparency resource for authorized AI-enabled devices, but it should not be read as a complete census of every AI tool used in care delivery.
- Policy sources support a governance/transparency story, not a claim that AI tools are clinically effective or universally deployed.

## W27 framing

- Reader-safe headline: policy attention is shifting toward transparency, disclosure, and governance infrastructure for health care AI.
- Distinguish FDA-regulated device software from ONC-certified health IT and from general workflow AI tools.
- Evidence limit: policy signals show rulemaking and oversight direction; they do not prove operational maturity.
