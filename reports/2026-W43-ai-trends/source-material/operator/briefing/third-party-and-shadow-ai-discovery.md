# Third-party and shadow AI discovery

## Editorial use

Use this file to make intake broader than formal AI projects. The report should explain that AI can enter healthcare through vendor products, cloud services, productivity tools, analytics platforms, outsourced workflows, and individual user behavior. Intake has to find those paths early enough to govern them.

## Source basis

- The Health Sector Coordinating Council third-party AI risk guide treats third-party AI as a health-sector governance, cybersecurity, procurement, and supply-chain issue.
- HSCC materials discuss governance committee review, vendor performance evaluation, monitoring, traceability, disclosure, human review, and escalation paths.
- HHS 405(d) / health-sector cybersecurity materials support attention to access, audit trails, third-party vendors, and operational resilience.
- AMA materials note that AI is used in medical devices, healthcare administration, and physician-burden reduction, and that policy and guidance are needed for both device and non-device healthcare AI.

## Reader-safe claims

- Intake should not wait for a tool to be labeled as an AI project. Embedded AI features and vendor updates can create AI-mediated work before governance teams see them.
- Third-party intake questions can include vendor role, data access, permitted use, model-update notification, audit rights, incident notification, output review, and renewal-time performance review.
- Shadow AI risk is not only cybersecurity risk. It can affect documentation, patient communication, operational decisions, billing, triage, scheduling, and clinician workload.
- Human review expectations should be captured when AI output could enter clinical documentation, patient communication, or consequential operational decisions.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not say a vendor caused harm, used data improperly, or bypassed governance unless staged evidence names and supports that claim.
- Do not rank vendors or product categories.
- Do not treat contract language or policy language as proof of real-world performance.
- Do not claim every generative AI use is prohibited or every use is acceptable.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support discovery and third-party-risk questions. They do not quantify shadow AI use, vendor risk frequency, or implementation maturity.
