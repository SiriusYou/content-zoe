# Drills, communication, and restart criteria

## Editorial use

Use this file to keep continuity planning from becoming a static document. The report should argue that fallback works only if people know when to use it, can practice it, can communicate status, and have criteria for restarting or narrowing AI-enabled use after disruption.

## Source basis

- ONC SAFER Guides are self-assessment tools intended to help organizations address safety and safe use of EHRs and respond to changing technology and practice conditions.
- HHS ransomware guidance and Security Rule materials support testing and revision of contingency plans and criticality analysis.
- NIST AI RMF supports lifecycle risk management, monitoring, roles, and responses to context-specific risk.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance announcement supports responsible adoption through local policies, monitoring, and governance.

## Reader-safe claims

- A fallback plan should include triggers, roles, communication channels, training expectations, practice or drill cadence, and restart criteria.
- Restart should be a decision, not only a technical event. The team may need to know which data was delayed, which actions were deferred, which messages or orders require reconciliation, and whether the AI feature should return at full or narrowed scope.
- Communication should reach the affected users: clinicians, staff, oversight teams, support teams, and in some contexts patients or caregivers.
- Testing and revision matter because workflows, staffing, interfaces, vendors, and AI capabilities change.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim drills guarantee readiness.
- Do not claim communication proves comprehension.
- Do not claim restart is safe just because the service is reachable again.
- Do not imply every incident requires public or patient-facing notice.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support questions about practice, communication, and restart. They do not show that any local downtime drill succeeded or that users understood the fallback under real pressure.
