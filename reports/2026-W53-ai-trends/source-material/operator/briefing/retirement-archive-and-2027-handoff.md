# Retirement archive and next-cycle handoff

## Retirement does not erase the governance record

The year-boundary carry-forward file should include retired, replaced, narrowed, or paused AI-enabled workflows when they still create review-relevant questions. Retirement may close active use, but it may not close every dependency.

The report may mention residual questions such as:

- data retention or access;
- patient or staff communication;
- user training or workflow undoing;
- vendor support or contract transition;
- fallback or replacement readiness;
- stored output or documentation created during use;
- unresolved monitoring, incident, or near-miss review;
- whether the item should stay visible in an archive rather than disappear.

Do not give legal advice about retention, contracts, disclosure, liability, or regulatory duties.

## Handoff into the next cycle

The carry-forward file should make the next review cycle easier to start. It can group items by next action:

- continue with owner confirmation;
- continue with monitoring review;
- request evidence or source-attribute update;
- validate locally;
- update use boundary or training;
- narrow or pause;
- retire or archive;
- reopen governance review;
- escalate for decision.

The report should make clear that these are possible categories, not universal requirements.

## What should remain visible

Unresolved items, deferred decisions, old conditions, and retired-state questions should remain visible until intentionally closed. The carry-forward file should show why an item is open and what would close it.

## Boundaries

Do not claim the handoff proves continuity, resilience, compliance, or maturity. It is a disciplined way to preserve open questions so they can be reviewed.
