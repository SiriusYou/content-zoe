## Facts and observations

- HHS AI materials connect responsible implementation with public trust, governance, and data infrastructure.
- HHS health-technology materials connect data liquidity, affordability, interoperability, and AI-enabled care.
- HealthIT.gov materials describe certification updates, information sharing, and algorithm transparency.
- TEFCA references provide national interoperability context.
- These sources support a control-surface story: transparency and exchange infrastructure can make AI governance more practical.

## W34 framing

- Reader-safe headline: data infrastructure and transparency work are governance prerequisites, not proof of AI value.
- Connect interoperability, certification, TEFCA, and algorithm transparency to reviewability and accountability.
- Avoid implying that infrastructure progress equals clinical success or validated AI performance.

## Evidence limits

- Federal infrastructure materials support direction and governance context.
- They do not establish implementation quality, patient outcomes, fairness, safety, or ROI.
