## Facts and observations

- Health-sector cybersecurity materials connect cyber resilience with patient safety and operational continuity.
- AI systems can introduce or amplify third-party, data-flow, contract, monitoring, incident-response, and business-continuity dependencies.
- NIST AI RMF provides governance vocabulary for identifying, measuring, managing, and governing AI risks.
- These references support treating AI procurement as ongoing risk management.
- They do not prove that any vendor, system, or organization is compliant, resilient, or effective.

## W34 framing

- Reader-safe headline: AI procurement is part of operational resilience.
- Treat vendor risk, cyber controls, monitoring, data rights, incident response, and contract discipline as healthcare AI governance topics.
- Avoid presenting framework references as proof that an organization can manage real failure modes.

## Evidence limits

- Cybersecurity and AI risk-framework materials are governance references.
- They do not validate vendor claims or prove operational maturity.
