## Facts and observations

- HIPAA and de-identification references define privacy boundaries and methods; they should not be used as shorthand for full AI readiness.
- NIST AI RMF provides cross-sector risk-management vocabulary.
- OECD and WHO materials emphasize trustworthy, human-centered, accountable, context-sensitive AI governance.
- These references support cautious scaling language and boundary-setting.
- They do not prove local compliance, equity, safety, effectiveness, or readiness.

## W34 framing

- Reader-safe headline: privacy and governance references define limits on AI scaling.
- Use them to keep claims narrow and accountable.
- Avoid converting framework language into evidence of deployment success.

## Evidence limits
