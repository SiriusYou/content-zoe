## Facts and observations

- FDA's AI-enabled device software materials focus on lifecycle management and marketing-submission recommendations for regulated device software.
- FDA maintains AI/ML-enabled medical-device and digital-health guidance materials that distinguish regulated device software from broader healthcare AI.
- FDA's public-comment request on real-world AI-enabled medical-device performance shows that post-deployment measurement remains a live evidence question.
- These materials support an assurance frame: oversight does not end at launch or clearance.
- They do not validate non-device workflow tools or prove that any particular product performs well in every clinical setting.

## W32 framing

- Reader-safe headline: regulated medical-device AI is moving toward lifecycle assurance and real-world monitoring.
- Separate regulated-device evidence from ordinary enterprise workflow AI.
- Treat real-world performance measurement as an unresolved but important governance problem.

## Evidence limits

- FDA materials support oversight direction and evidence-question framing.
- They do not establish universal product performance or non-device AI validation.
