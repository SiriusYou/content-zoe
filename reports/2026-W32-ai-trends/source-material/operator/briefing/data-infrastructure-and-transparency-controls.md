## Facts and observations

- HHS connects health technology leadership to data liquidity, affordability, interoperability, and an AI-enabled healthcare system.
- TEFCA is described as national interoperability infrastructure with large-scale health-record exchange.
- HHS AI strategy material emphasizes governance, public trust, and responsible implementation.
- ONC/HealthIT.gov materials connect certification program updates, information sharing, and algorithm transparency.
- These materials describe infrastructure and governance levers; they do not validate clinical AI products or outcomes.

## W32 framing

- Reader-safe headline: infrastructure makes AI easier to deploy and easier to govern, but it is not the same as proof of performance.
- Use data liquidity, TEFCA, certification, and algorithm transparency as control-surface evidence.
- Avoid implying that interoperability progress by itself proves AI safety, fairness, or ROI.

## Evidence limits

- Federal infrastructure sources support direction and policy context.
- They do not establish implementation quality, patient outcomes, or product-level performance.
