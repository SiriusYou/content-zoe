## Facts and observations

- AMA reports broad professional AI use among physicians in 2026.
- AMA's augmented-intelligence framing emphasizes support for physicians and care teams rather than replacement of professional judgment.
- AMA materials keep training needs, workflow integration, patient use, and skill-loss concerns in view.
- These sources support a claim that physician exposure creates governance and education pressure.
- They do not prove audited deployment quality, clinical benefit, productivity ROI, or patient-outcome improvement.

## W32 framing

- Reader-safe headline: physician adoption is no longer just a novelty signal; it creates an assurance and training obligation.
- Connect adoption pressure to education, review, escalation, and human accountability.
- Avoid treating self-reported use as validation of specific tools or broad outcomes.

## Evidence limits

- The survey is adoption and sentiment evidence.
- It does not establish that any particular AI tool is safe, effective, equitable, or independently validated.
