## Facts and observations

- FDA AI-enabled device software materials support treating AI as a lifecycle-management problem, not a one-time launch decision.
- Predetermined change control planning creates a vocabulary for anticipated modifications, modification protocols, performance evaluation, and bounded change.
- Lifecycle management materials support monitoring and postmarket discipline for regulated device software.
- These sources are useful as a disciplined example for healthcare AI change governance.
- They do not mean every workflow AI tool is regulated the same way as AI-enabled medical device software.

## W35 framing

- Reader-safe headline direction: AI governance needs change control, not just initial approval.
- Use FDA device-software materials as a concrete lifecycle-control example.
- Keep the boundary explicit: device software regulation is not a blanket validation of every healthcare AI product.

## Evidence limits

- These sources are strongest for regulated device-software lifecycle discipline.
- They do not establish safety or effectiveness for non-device workflow tools, ambient documentation products, administrative models, or local deployments.
