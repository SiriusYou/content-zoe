## Facts and observations

- HSCC third-party AI risk materials support treating healthcare AI as a supply-chain and vendor-risk problem.
- The materials connect AI governance to vendor inventories, contract terms, testing evidence, cybersecurity review, data flows, monitoring, and incident response.
- HICP and 405(d) materials support a health-sector cyber-resilience frame: cyber risk can affect patient safety and operational continuity.
- NIST AI RMF provides cross-sector vocabulary for governing, mapping, measuring, and managing AI risk.
- These sources support claims that AI procurement and vendor management are part of the operating model.
- They do not prove any specific vendor is safe, any contract is sufficient, or any organization is resilient.

## W35 framing

- Reader-safe headline direction: healthcare AI assurance depends on vendor evidence and operational resilience.
- Connect third-party AI risk, supply-chain transparency, cyber controls, and incident response to reader-facing accountability.
- Avoid framing a named framework or guide as proof of operational maturity.

## Evidence limits

- These sources support governance requirements and risk categories.
- They do not validate vendor claims, local controls, deployment quality, or resilience under real incidents.
