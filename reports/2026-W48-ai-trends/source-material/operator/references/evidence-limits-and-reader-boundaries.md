# Evidence limits and reader boundaries

## Editorial use

Use this reference to keep the W48 report cautious. The report should offer a governance frame: "Healthcare AI should earn its renewal." It must not offer a factual weekly market roundup, vendor ranking, product review, contract interpretation, legal/regulatory opinion, or proof that any renewal process works.

## What the sources support

- Health IT selection, upgrading, implementation, maintenance, and contracting are meaningful governance moments.
- AI-enabled workflows should be reviewed across lifecycle context, actual use, monitoring, support, updates, dependencies, local validation, and ownership.
- Renewal can function as a checkpoint for evidence refresh, vendor support review, approved-use comparison, change review, fallback review, and exit readiness.
- Contract resources and vendor evidence can help frame questions, but they are not legal advice and do not replace local review.
- Continued-use decisions can include continue, narrow, pause, reopen validation, replace, retire, or transition.

## What the sources do not support

- No claim that any specific AI workflow, product, vendor, hospital, or health system should be renewed or not renewed.
- No claim that renewal review, contract checkpoints, vendor evidence, monitoring records, support tickets, training updates, or decision records prove safety, compliance, fairness, cybersecurity readiness, patient trust, outcome improvement, or operational maturity.
- No claim that a contract or support commitment resolves local workflow, data, clinical, privacy, security, or vendor-dependency risk.
- No legal advice about contracts, FDA, ONC, HIPAA, malpractice, procurement, cybersecurity, professional responsibility, or patient communication.

## Required evidence warnings

The final report should preserve two evidence-grade warnings in each locale, but each report file should use only its own language:

1. A leading warning that the draft is limited to staged procurement, health IT implementation, risk-management, governance, and professional-source material and does not include local contract terms, utilization logs, performance metrics, incident records, legal analysis, or named implementation proof.
2. A mid-report warning near the discussion of renewal review, vendor evidence, monitoring records, support history, and contract checkpoints noting that these practices can make continued use more reviewable but do not prove safety, compliance, fairness, cybersecurity readiness, patient trust, outcome improvement, or operational maturity.

Locale rule:

- `report.en.md` must include only the English `EVIDENCE_GRADE_WARN` comments.
- `report.zh.md` must include only the Chinese `EVIDENCE_GRADE_WARN` comments.
- Do not duplicate both languages into both report files.

`EVIDENCE_GRADE_WARN: This draft is limited to staged procurement, health IT implementation, risk-management, governance, and professional-source material. It does not include local contract terms, utilization logs, performance metrics, incident records, legal analysis, or named implementation proof.`

## Reader promise

The report may argue that healthcare AI continued use should be re-earned at renewal. It must not claim that the sector has solved renewal review, vendor accountability, procurement evidence, lifecycle monitoring, or exit readiness.
