# Vendor evidence and contract checkpoints

## Editorial use

Use this file to connect renewal with vendor dependency without giving legal advice. The report should ask what evidence, support, update notice, data handling, audit access, and exit information should be reviewed before an AI-enabled workflow is renewed.

## Source basis

- ONC EHR contracting and vendor-selection resources support asking about vendor support, implementation, training, interfaces, total cost, terms, and risk management, while making clear that contract resources are not legal advice.
- ONC selecting/upgrading health IT materials describe requirements, vendor interaction, and risk management in health IT acquisition and upgrading.
- NIST AI RMF supports managing risks across organizations, system lifecycle, and third-party contexts.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance announcement supports local governance and monitoring as part of responsible AI adoption.

## Reader-safe claims

- Renewal review can ask what the vendor changed, what support was provided, what evidence was updated, what data access or retention terms apply, and what notices the customer received.
- Contracts, implementation records, support tickets, update notices, and service records can help define what to ask at renewal.
- Vendor assurances should be treated as inputs to local review, not as substitutes for local governance.
- Renewal is a chance to revisit exit rights, transition support, data return or deletion expectations, audit access, incident notice, and model or feature change notice.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not interpret contract terms or give legal advice.
- Do not claim a contract makes an AI workflow safe or compliant.
- Do not rank vendors or products.
- Do not imply all vendor dependencies are unacceptable.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support vendor and contract-review questions. They do not include local contract language, support records, service levels, vendor notices, or legal conclusions.
