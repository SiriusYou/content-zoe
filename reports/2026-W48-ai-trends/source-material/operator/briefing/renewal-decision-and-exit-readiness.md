# Renewal decision and exit readiness

## Editorial use

Use this file to keep renewal from becoming a rubber stamp. The report should argue that renewal should be a decision with options: continue, narrow, pause, reopen validation, replace, retire, or prepare transition.

## Source basis

- NIST AI RMF supports managing risks through decisions and actions across the AI lifecycle.
- Joint Commission/CHAI responsible-use guidance announcement supports local governance and monitoring in responsible adoption.
- WHO health AI guidance supports accountability and protection of people affected by AI.
- ONC selecting/upgrading and contract resources support risk-aware health IT selection and contract planning, without substituting for legal advice.

## Reader-safe claims

- Renewal should name the decision, the evidence considered, the unresolved gaps, the owner, the next review date, and the conditions that would trigger earlier review.
- If the tool is continued, the record should say whether continued use is full-scope or narrowed.
- If the tool is paused, replaced, or retired, the organization needs transition, communication, data, support, and fallback plans.
- Exit readiness makes renewal more credible because it shows the organization is not locked into continued use by default.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim exit readiness proves resilience.
- Do not imply replacement is automatically better than renewal.
- Do not claim all renewal decisions should end in retirement.
- Do not give legal advice about termination, renewal, data return, or contract remedies.

## Evidence limit

The staged sources support decision-record and exit-readiness questions. They do not include local transition plans, renewal approvals, or contract terms.
