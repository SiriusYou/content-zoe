# Device list and public summary limits

## Editorial use

Use this file to explain that public AI-device lists can improve visibility but should not be overread. The report should use the FDA AI-enabled medical device list as an example of structured public visibility, while keeping the limits explicit.

## Source basis to cite cautiously

- FDA's AI-enabled medical devices page says the list is intended to identify AI-enabled medical devices authorized for marketing in the United States.
- FDA says the list can help healthcare providers and patients identify when medical devices use AI technologies.
- FDA states the list is not comprehensive and is based primarily on AI-related terms in marketing authorization summaries and/or device classification.
- FDA says public database entries include releasable information and that summaries are not all-inclusive.
- FDA says it will continue updating the list periodically and has discussed exploring methods to identify devices incorporating foundation-model functionality in future updates.

## Supported claims

- Public lists can improve basic visibility by making some AI-enabled device authorizations easier to find.
- A list entry should be treated as a starting point for review, not as a complete dossier.
- Governance teams still need to inspect intended use, indications, local workflow fit, public summary limits, monitoring expectations, and local validation needs.
- A public AI-device list can help with inventory discipline when combined with local portfolio records.

## Unsafe claims

- Do not claim every AI medical device appears on the FDA list.
- Do not treat inclusion on the list as proof of local safety or effectiveness.
- Do not claim foundation-model functionality is already completely tagged or classified in public lists.
- Do not rank vendors or devices.

## Evidence warning

The staged sources support public-device-list visibility as one transparency input. They do not provide enough evidence to evaluate a specific device's local deployment, comparative quality, or ongoing performance.
