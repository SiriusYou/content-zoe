# Lifecycle risk and change records

## Editorial use

Use this file to tie transparency to lifecycle governance. The report should explain that transparency can decay if it is not updated when the model, data, interface, intended use, workflow, vendor support, or local monitoring signals change.

## Source basis to cite cautiously

- FDA's AI in Software as a Medical Device page describes the development, deployment, use, and maintenance of AI technologies as complex lifecycle processes that benefit from careful management.
- FDA's final guidance on predetermined change control plans for AI-enabled device software functions provides recommendations for PCCPs in marketing submissions for AI-enabled devices.
- FDA/Health Canada/MHRA PCCP guiding principles discuss planned modifications, validation methods, impact assessment, monitoring, and ways to revert or stop changes that fail specified performance criteria.
- NIST AI RMF 1.0 organizes AI risk management around Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions and is intended for organizations designing, developing, deploying, or using AI systems.
- NIST's AI RMF page notes ongoing profile work for critical infrastructure AI risk management as of 2026, underscoring that AI risk management continues to be operationalized in high-consequence settings.

## Supported claims

- Transparency should include change context: what changed, why it changed, who approved it, what evidence was checked, what monitoring will follow, and what escalation or rollback path exists.
- PCCP concepts are directly relevant to regulated AI-enabled device software functions and can be used cautiously as an example of planned-change discipline.
- NIST AI RMF supports treating transparency, measurement, risk response, and documentation as lifecycle activities rather than one-time launch artifacts.

## Unsafe claims

- Do not claim PCCP guidance governs non-device operational AI.
- Do not claim a change record proves safety, effectiveness, fairness, or compliance.
- Do not claim a specific model update occurred unless staged evidence names it.
- Do not treat NIST AI RMF adoption as proof of mature implementation.

## Evidence warning

The staged sources support lifecycle transparency and change-record discipline. They do not prove that any specific AI-enabled healthcare workflow has been updated, validated, paused, rolled back, or improved.
