# Assurance and local implementation context

## Editorial use

Use this file to keep the report grounded in local implementation. National guidance can define transparency and assurance questions, but local organizations still have to decide whether the tool fits their patient population, workflow, staff responsibilities, escalation paths, and monitoring capacity.

## Source basis to cite cautiously

- CHAI's Assurance Standards Guide organizes health AI considerations across the lifecycle and principle themes including usefulness, fairness, safety, transparency, and security/privacy.
- CHAI's assurance-lab materials describe evaluation across pre-deployment, implementation, and post-deployment/monitoring phases.
- The Joint Commission and CHAI announced responsible-use AI guidance in 2025 for internal governance by U.S. health systems.
- WHO ethics and governance guidance emphasizes accountability, transparency, inclusiveness, responsiveness, and sustainability in health AI governance.
- NIST AI RMF supports context mapping and risk management across organizations that design, deploy, or use AI systems.

## Supported claims

- Assurance is stronger when it includes local workflow review, local data and population considerations, post-deployment monitoring, and accountable ownership.
- National frameworks can help define the questions, but local governance must still answer them for each workflow.
- Transparency should include who owns follow-up, who can escalate concerns, and how local feedback is incorporated.

## Unsafe claims

- Do not claim CHAI, Joint Commission, WHO, or NIST guidance certifies any specific local AI use.
- Do not claim local validation has occurred unless staged evidence supplies it.
- Do not imply assurance labs or external review are universally available, required, or sufficient.

## Evidence warning

The staged sources support assurance and local-context review as governance duties. They do not provide local deployment data, local validation results, patient outcome data, or named case evidence.
