# Source attributes and predictive DSI records

## Editorial use

Use this file to connect healthcare AI transparency to source attributes and risk-management records. The report should explain that source attributes can make algorithmic decision support more inspectable, while still warning that inspectability is not the same as proof of local performance.

## Source basis to cite cautiously

- ONC's HTI-1 final rule page describes algorithm transparency requirements for AI and predictive algorithms that are part of certified health IT.
- HealthIT.gov's decision support interventions test method describes source attributes as technical performance and quality information for evidence-based and predictive decision support interventions.
- The test method says certified health IT modules must support access to complete and up-to-date plain-language source attribute information for identified users in covered contexts.
- The test method also describes intervention risk management for predictive DSIs supplied by the developer as part of the health IT module, including risk analysis, mitigation practices, and governance controls.
- ONC frames transparency information as helping clinical users assess fairness, appropriateness, validity, effectiveness, and safety.

## Supported claims

- Source attributes can give governance teams a baseline vocabulary for asking what an intervention is, who developed it, what data or evidence it relies on, what demographic variables are used, and what risk practices apply.
- A predictive DSI transparency record is more useful when it is current, plain-language, accessible to the right users, and connected to risk management.
- "Information not available" can be a meaningful governance signal when the applicable rule permits or requires that status to be indicated.

## Unsafe claims

- Do not claim HTI-1 covers every healthcare AI tool.
- Do not claim source attributes prove an intervention is fair, valid, effective, or safe.
- Do not imply a certified module's technical capability means all third-party predictive DSIs have complete information.
- Do not claim legal compliance from staged material alone.

## Evidence warning

The staged sources support source attributes as a transparency mechanism for specified certified-health-IT contexts. They do not prove that any local organization has complete records, that users understand those records, or that the records validate the intervention in local practice.
